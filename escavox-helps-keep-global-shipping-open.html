Making Waves · 7 February 2023

Helping keep global shipping open

Escavox technology is proving particularly significant in an environment where exporters are being forced to lean on sea freight as their key mode of transport while air travel remains heavily restricted.

Real-time visibility across long ocean journeys helps keep global food supply chains moving — and gives exporters the confidence to switch modes without losing sight of quality.

Our story

The intelligence layer for global fresh produce

Escavox is the Cold Chain Intelligence Engine for fresh produce. We turn the journey of every shipment into commercial intelligence that protects shelf life, helping growers, exporters, importers and retailers know what happened, understand why, and predict what's next.

Where it began

Why we built Escavox.

The problem was never measuring the temperature. It was what you do with what you learn.

Tracking food isn't new. Time-and-temperature clauses have sat in supply contracts for decades, alongside chilled warehouses and cold chains.

Our founders, Luke, Nici and Chris, had seen the problem from all three sides of the chain: as retailers, carriers and producers, because they'd worked inside each of them. When produce is lost, accountability falls to whoever can prove what happened, so the data became a shield, used defensively, after the fact, to settle claims.

The chains that work look different. Where partners align around shared, trusted data, above all shelf-life data, they waste less and deliver better. Producers see steadier demand and fewer rejections. Carriers get predictability. Retailers lose less. Everyone is more profitable.

The opportunity

The difference data makes.

Where shelf life is measured and shared, loss stays low. Where it isn't, produce pays the price, the same journey, a very different outcome.

Close that gap, give fresh produce the same class of data, and you change how fresh food moves through the world. That's the platform Escavox set out to build.

That vision hasn't changed since day one, only strengthened: Escavox as the data platform the whole fresh food industry can trust, one source of truth for everyone moving produce across the globe. Because people will always need to eat, and fresh food will always spoil.

Sustainability

We only have one planet

We're doing our part to look after our home. By protecting produce quality and designing inefficiency out of the cold chain, Escavox helps cut food waste and food miles.

Food waste

Roughly a third of all food produced is lost or wasted. Protecting quality in transit keeps more of it on the shelf and out of landfill.

Food miles

Food miles account for around 6% of all global emissions. Better routing and fewer failed loads mean less wasted transport.

Reusable devices

Our multi-use trackers return to the pool and are re-deployed journey after journey, keeping hardware working and plastic out of landfill.

Escavox is a proud sponsor of End Food Waste Australia and works with industry bodies and R&D programs, because only by learning together can we effect change.

Quality is sustainability

Every load that arrives in spec is waste avoided

Supplying food at optimum quality is the key to reducing costs and eradicating waste. When produce is rejected or downgraded, the emissions of growing, packing and moving it are wasted too.

Cold Chain Intelligence pinpoints exactly where quality is being lost, so you fix the root cause, not just the symptom.

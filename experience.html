The unbroken chain

Quality is lost at a link — and the link is knowable.

Losses rarely happen everywhere at once. They happen at a single handover: a warm dock, a delayed precool, a reefer set a degree wrong. Escavox shows you which one — and what it cost.

Inside the container

Position-level behaviour, not a single average.

Temperature is never uniform. Pallets at the doors run warm; the delivery end runs cold. Escavox resolves the field across pallet positions — dwell, stratification and door-end exposure — so you act on the pallet at risk, not the mean that hides it.

Exception-first

Thousands of journeys in. The few that need you, out.

No team can watch every load. Escavox does — and surfaces only the shipments crossing a threshold, so your people spend their time on exceptions, not spreadsheets.

The journey profile

The measures that decide shelf life, made physical.

Vapour pressure deficit, dew point spread and transpiration — read continuously across the journey. When the spread collapses, condensation forms and quality begins to go. Escavox sees it as it happens, not in the post-mortem.

The network effect

A network that compounds.

Every grower, exporter and importer on Escavox sharpens the picture for the next. One relationship opens another, across countries and commodities — cold chain intelligence for the global fresh produce supply chain.

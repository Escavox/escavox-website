Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Draft for review. This policy is a working template. Please have it reviewed by a legal adviser and complete the registered entity details before relying on it.

This Privacy Policy explains how Escavox ("Escavox", "we", "us") handles personal information collected through this website. It covers the marketing website only; use of the My Tracks™ platform and Escavox devices is governed by the agreement between Escavox and each customer.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you:

  • Enquiry details you provide through our contact form — your name, work email, company (optional), region (optional) and your message.
  • Technical data that any website receives automatically, such as your IP address and browser type, held in standard server logs by our hosting provider.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track your activity across other websites. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the information or contact you've requested.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.
  • To maintain the security and proper operation of the website.

Our lawful basis for this is your consent (given when you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our products and services.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you, including:

  • Web3Forms, which processes contact-form submissions and delivers them to us by email.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website's typefaces; your browser requests these from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Our email and hosting providers, used to receive and store your enquiry.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

International transfers

Escavox operates in Australia and South Africa and works with international partners, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where it is, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — under the Australian Privacy Act, South Africa's POPIA, the EU/UK GDPR or other applicable law — you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict its use. To make a request, email us at info@escavox.com.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version and its date are shown at the top of this page.

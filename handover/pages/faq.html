FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Our system is simple to access and use — but you may want to know more. Here are the questions we're asked most often.

Which Escavox devices are available?

The flagship real-time tracker is the always-on Escavox G6+ (TT19), which adds shock detection and runs up to 5 months per charge (rechargeable), with an 8-month transmission life. The G6 (TT19) shares the same on-device display and is manually armed with a long-press of the start button before dispatch. Both continuously record temperature, humidity, light, movement and location — and are PPECB and EU citrus certified. The range also includes the reusable G4 and the lightweight Escavox Lite (USB). All feed the My Tracks™ platform. See the range →

Do I have to switch it on?

It depends on the model. The G6+ and G4 are always-on — active before they reach your shipment, so there's no risk of a device being left switched off. The G6 and Escavox Lite (USB) are manually activated: a quick long-press of the start button before you pack them with the consignment. Either way it takes seconds, and where there's an on-device display it confirms the device is recording.

What does it record, and how accurate is it?

Temperature, humidity, light, movement, shock and location. Temperature is measured to ±0.2°C and relative humidity to ±2%, across a wide range of -20°C to +65°C — suitable for chilled and frozen produce alike.

When is the data available?

In real time. Using global LTE connectivity (5G/4G with 2G fallback), the device conveys data as your product moves. You can view it on a secure, easy-to-read dashboard via PC, smartphone or tablet, wherever you are in the world.

How long does the battery last?

The G6+ runs up to 5 months on a single charge and is rechargeable, with a total transmission life of 8 months — so one device covers many journeys. The G6 has a battery and transmission life of 120 days.

Can I use it for air freight and export?

Yes. The G6+ has a Flight-Safe Mode fully compliant with IATA regulations, and is PPECB certified — approved for all fresh produce export loads, including EU citrus.

What happens to the device after a journey?

It depends on the device. The multi-use G4 is returned from the destination and re-enters the Escavox pool for re-deployment — earning a returns rebate and keeping plastic out of landfill. The single-use G6+ and G6 are not returned for re-deployment and are recycled responsibly at end of life.

Is my data secure, and are there platform fees?

Your tracking data is automatically collected and curated on a secure platform. There are no platform fees — you get unrestricted access to your data and performance metrics, and you can choose to share data with supply chain partners inside the secure environment.

How do I get started?

Get in touch and our team will onboard you — despatching devices to your nominated pack locations, configured to your commodity and temperature profile. It's rapid, hassle-free, zero capex and zero tech. You could start tracking tomorrow.

I'm holding a tracker — how do I check its data?

If you're a customer or importer holding an Escavox device and want to check its data, contact our team and we'll point you to the right dashboard.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Draft for review. This reflects how the website is built today (no tracking cookies). If analytics or marketing tools are added later, this policy and the consent approach must be revisited.

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device. This page explains how cookies are used on the Escavox website.

The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not build a profile of you or follow you across other sites. Because we don't set non-essential cookies, no cookie-consent banner is required to browse the site.

What we do and don't use

  • No analytics cookies, we don't run Google Analytics or any similar tool on this site.
  • No advertising or social-tracking cookies.
  • Third-party requests. Some content loads from other providers as you use the site, the website's typefaces come from Google Fonts, and the contact form is processed by Web3Forms when you submit it. These providers receive technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver that content. See our Privacy Policy for detail.

If you follow a link to another Escavox service, for example the My Tracks™ sign-in or the Escavox app, that service may use its own cookies, covered by its own terms.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time, and set your browser to block them. As this site doesn't rely on non-essential cookies, doing so won't affect how it works.

Changes

If we introduce cookies in future, for example website analytics, we'll update this policy and add a consent mechanism where the law requires one.

Built for real-world cold chains

End-to-end visibility on every shipment

Global cold chains are complex, multi-party and fragmented, shipments pass through many hands, modes and facilities, often without a unified view of what actually happens.

The problem

Fragmented, invisible hand-offs

Data is scattered across systems, stakeholders and hand-offs. When quality issues occur, teams are left guessing where things went wrong, when conditions changed, and who had custody at the time.

The solution

One coherent view of the journey

Each journey is automatically broken into discrete legs, so teams see how conditions evolve across transport modes, facilities and hand-offs, turning fragmented data into clear, actionable insight.

Continuous capture

Every signal that affects quality

Escavox devices capture the full condition of your load in real time, so you understand not just what happened, but where, when and why.

Temperature

The core of cold chain integrity, tracked to ±0.3°C across the entire journey.

Humidity

Relative humidity to ±3%, critical for weight loss, wilting and quality.

Light

Detects when a load is opened or exposed, a proxy for handling events.

Movement

Motion signatures that reveal dwell, transfers and idle time along the route.

Location

Global positioning that maps every leg from point of pack to destination.

Shock G6+

Impact events that can bruise or damage sensitive produce, on the G6+.

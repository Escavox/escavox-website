FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Our system is simple to access and use — but you may want to know more. Here are the questions we're asked most often.

Which Escavox devices are available?

There's a device for every shipment, so you can match the tracker to the load. The G6+ (TT19) is the flagship real-time tracker — always-on, with an on-device display, shock detection, up to 5 months per charge (rechargeable) and an 8-month transmission life. The G6 (TT19) gives you the same real-time tracking and display but is manually armed with a long-press before dispatch, with a 120-day battery and transmission life. The G4 is a rugged, reusable real-time tracker — returned and re-deployed journey after journey for a lower cost per trip. And the Escavox USB (TempU08) is a lightweight plug-in logger for when a full real-time tracker isn't required, generating PDF & CSV reports the moment it's plugged in. The G-series stream live to My Tracks™; the USB logger is read on plug-in. See the range →

Do I have to switch it on?

It depends on the device. The G6+ and G4 are always-on — active before they reach your shipment, so there's no risk of one being left switched off. The G6 and Escavox USB are manually activated: a quick long-press of the start button before you pack them with the consignment (the USB has a 30-minute start delay so it's stable before recording). Either way it takes seconds, and where there's an on-device display it confirms the device is recording.

What does it record, and how accurate is it?

The real-time trackers — G6+, G6 and G4 — record temperature, humidity, light, movement, shock and location, with temperature to ±0.2°C and humidity to ±2% across -20°C to +65°C. The Escavox USB logger records temperature to ±0.5°C (0.1°C resolution) across a wider -30°C to +60°C range — so there's an option suited to chilled and frozen produce alike.

When is the data available?

For the real-time trackers (G6+, G6 and G4), in real time — using global LTE connectivity (5G/4G with 2G fallback), the device streams data as your product moves, viewable on a secure, easy-to-read dashboard via PC, smartphone or tablet, wherever you are in the world. The Escavox USB logger isn't live: it generates PDF and CSV reports the moment it's plugged in — no software required.

How long does the battery last?

It varies by device. The G6+ runs up to 5 months on a single charge, is rechargeable, and has an 8-month transmission life. The G6 has a 120-day battery and transmission life. The reusable G4 is returned and re-deployed across many journeys, and the Escavox USB logger holds up to 32,000 readings with a ~2-year shelf life.

Can I use it for air freight and export?

Yes. The real-time trackers (G6+, G6 and G4) have a Flight-Safe Mode compliant with IATA regulations and are PPECB certified — approved for fresh produce export loads, including EU citrus. The Escavox USB logger is FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliant for regulated reporting.

What happens to the device after a journey?

It depends on the device. The multi-use G4 is returned from the destination and re-enters the Escavox pool for re-deployment — earning a returns rebate and keeping plastic out of landfill. The single-use G6+, G6 and Escavox USB are not returned for re-deployment and are recycled responsibly at end of life.

Is my data secure, and are there platform fees?

Your tracking data is automatically collected and curated on a secure platform. There are no platform fees — you get unrestricted access to your data and performance metrics, and you can choose to share data with supply chain partners inside the secure environment.

How do I get started?

Get in touch and our team will onboard you — despatching devices to your nominated pack locations, configured to your commodity and temperature profile. It's rapid, hassle-free, zero capex and zero tech. You could start tracking tomorrow.

I'm holding a tracker — how do I check its data?

If you're holding an Escavox device, download the Escavox app and use Scan a tracker, or get your data at scan.escavox.com. Not sure which shipment it belongs to? Contact our team and we'll point you to the right dashboard.

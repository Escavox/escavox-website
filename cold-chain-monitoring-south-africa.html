South Africa & Southern Africa

Cold chain monitoring for South African fresh produce exports

From packhouses in the Western Cape, Limpopo and the Eastern Cape to markets in the UK, Europe, the Middle East and Asia, Escavox tracks the temperature and humidity of every shipment and turns that data into shelf-life decisions — with a local team in Cape Town.

Built for your commodities

The fruit South Africa ships, modelled per commodity.

Shelf life isn't generic. A navel orange, a Crimson Seedless grape and a Hass avocado lose life at different rates under the same conditions — so Escavox models each commodity on its own physics.

Citrus

The world's second-largest citrus exporter ships through long reefer legs and strict protocol markets. Track steri regimes and cold treatment compliance from packhouse to discharge port.

Table grapes

From the Hex River, Berg River and Orange River regions to UK and EU retail programmes — see exactly where cold chain breaks threaten condition and SO₂ performance.

Avocados

Ripening is temperature history. Know the true accumulated heat of every consignment before it reaches the ripening room, not after the complaint.

Berries

The most unforgiving commodity in the chain. Hours matter — real-time visibility from the first chill to final-mile delivery protects premium punnets.

Stone fruit

Dual-temperature regimes and chilling-injury windows, monitored instead of assumed, across every pallet position in the container.

Pome fruit

Apples and pears from Ceres and the Langkloof carry months of storage life — if the chain holds. Verify it does, shipment by shipment.

Your trade lanes

Visibility where South African exporters need it most.

Port congestion, vessel delays and transhipment hand-offs are where loads are won or lost. Escavox follows the fruit itself — not the paperwork — through every link.

UK & Europe

The long haul north — through Cape Town, Gqeberha and Durban, across the equator, into retail programmes with firm specs and firmer arrival windows.

Middle East & Asia

Growing protocol markets with cold-treatment requirements where a verifiable, independent temperature record is the difference between cleared and rejected.

Regional & cross-border

Road freight into and out of Zimbabwe and Namibia, and the domestic leg from farm to port — often the least visible, most damaging kilometres of the journey.

How it works

In the carton at the packhouse. Intelligence by arrival.

1. Place a device

An Escavox tracker travels inside the carton, recording what the fruit actually experiences — not what the container setpoint claims.

2. Follow the journey

Temperature, humidity and location stream to MyTracks™ through port, vessel and discharge — no wiring, no pairing, no setup at the packhouse.

3. Act on shelf life

Every shipment ranked by commercial risk, shelf life quantified per commodity, and a verifiable record every partner in the chain can trust.

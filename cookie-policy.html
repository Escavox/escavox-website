Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Draft for review. This reflects how the website is built today: Google Analytics runs only if you accept analytics cookies.

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device. This page explains how cookies are used on the Escavox website.

The short version

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, but only if you accept it in the cookie banner. If you decline or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are set. We don't use advertising or social-tracking cookies.

What we do and don't use

  • Analytics cookies (optional). With your consent, Google Analytics 4 sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* (kept for up to 2 years) to count visits and see which pages are used, and records when an enquiry is sent from the contact form. The data is processed by Google; we don't use it to identify you or for advertising.
  • Your cookie choice. Your accept/decline choice is stored in your browser's local storage (escavox-consent) so we don't ask on every page. It isn't sent to us.
  • No advertising or social-tracking cookies.
  • Third-party requests. Some content loads from other providers as you use the site, the website's typefaces come from Google Fonts, and the contact form is processed by Web3Forms when you submit it. These providers receive technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver that content. See our Privacy Policy for detail.

If you follow a link to another Escavox service, for example the My Tracks™ sign-in or the Escavox app, that service may use its own cookies, covered by its own terms.

Changing your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page). If you withdraw consent, we delete the analytics cookies from this site. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings; the site works fully without them.

Changes

If we change how we use cookies, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

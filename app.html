The Escavox app lets you…

Link data on the run

Add your order number, customer, shipping information, SKU details and photos to any track, anywhere, anytime.

Let customers add data too

Your customers can add photos of the product on arrival. Link data even when offline — the phone stores it and uploads when back in range.

Get alerts on your phone

Receive alerts as notifications directly to your phone the moment a shipment crosses a threshold.

Manage notifications

Subscribe or unsubscribe to phone and email notifications, per shipment or across your network.

Jump to the track behind an alert

Open the exact track that generated the alert and see what happened, in context, straight away.

Stay in sync everywhere

Everything you add on the app flows straight into My Tracks™ — one source of truth across your team.
